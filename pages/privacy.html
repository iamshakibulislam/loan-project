Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Atlas Fund Capital ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of the businesses and individuals who interact with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through atlasfundcapital.com and our application and contact forms, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or submitting an inquiry, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly to us, such as your name, company name, email address, phone number, and details about your business and financing needs. We may also collect limited technical information automatically, including your IP address, browser type, referring URL, and pages visited, to operate and improve our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to review your inquiry, identify financing options that may fit your business, contact you about your request, respond to your questions, and comply with legal and regulatory obligations. We may also use it to send you information we believe is relevant to your financing needs, subject to the communication preferences you select.

4. SMS / Text Messaging Program

By opting in through a checkbox on our website application form, you consent to receive text messages from Atlas Fund Capital. Depending on the boxes you select, these may include transactional messages related to your business financing inquiry (for example, application updates and scheduling a call with your advisor) and/or marketing messages. You may opt in to either, both, or neither — consent is not a condition of obtaining services. SMS opt-in is provided separately from email consent and is never pre-checked.

  • ✓ Message frequency may vary.
  • ✓ Message and data rates may apply.
  • ✓ Reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages.
  • ✓ Reply HELP for help, or contact us at contact@atlasfundcapital.com.
  • ✓ Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

After you reply STOP, we will send a single confirmation message and will not send further texts unless you opt in again. Opting out of text messages will not unsubscribe you from email communications, and vice versa.

Mobile information sharing. We will not share or sell your mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes. Your mobile opt-in data and consent to receive text messages will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Information may be shared with service providers and carriers solely to the extent necessary to deliver the text messaging program on our behalf, and they are obligated to protect it.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information with the funding institutions and service providers involved in evaluating or facilitating your request, and with vendors who help us operate our website and communications, only to the extent necessary to provide those services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights.

6. Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, measure the effectiveness of our website, and understand how visitors use our pages. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the website.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Access

You may opt out of text messages by replying STOP, unsubscribe from emails using the link provided in those messages, or contact us to update or request deletion of your information. To exercise any of these choices, email us at contact@atlasfundcapital.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us at contact@atlasfundcapital.com or by mail at Atlas Fund Capital, 444 Alaska Avenue Suite #BWM188, Torrance, CA 90503. See also our Terms & Conditions.
