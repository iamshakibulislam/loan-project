# Lead / Application Form
- The lead/application form must be single page, single step — all fields on one page with one Submit button. Do NOT build a multi-step/wizard form. Confidence: 0.9
- The application form lives on its own dedicated /apply/ page. The home page hero should contain only an "Apply Now" button linking to /apply/ — not the form itself. Confidence: 0.9
- Form fields use text inputs (type manually) — not select dropdowns — except for time-in-business which remains a dropdown. All fields are required. Confidence: 0.70
- Communication consent uses separate, independent checkboxes: one for transactional SMS, one for marketing SMS, and one for email. SMS consent must be distinct from email consent, and marketing SMS must have its own standalone checkbox separate from other use cases. Confidence: 0.85
- All consent/opt-in checkboxes must be optional and unchecked by default (never pre-checked); consent is not a condition of obtaining services. Confidence: 0.85
- Each SMS opt-in checkbox must carry the required disclaimers: brand name, use case (transactional/marketing), "Reply STOP to opt out", "Reply HELP for help", "standard message and data rates may apply", "message frequency may vary", and hyperlinks to Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. Confidence: 0.85
- The opt-in form must be branded with the same brand being registered and include the business name within the consent text. Confidence: 0.80
- Maintain a Privacy Policy page containing the carrier-required mobile-data verbiage: "We will not share or sell your mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes." Confidence: 0.80
